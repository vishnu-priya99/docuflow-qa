Design Review Meeting Notes - ECO-4471
Date: 2024-04-18

Attendees: R&D Lead (Priya), Quality Engineer (Arjun), Regulatory Affairs (Meera)

Discussion: Reviewed the proposed engineering change to increase the luer
connector wall thickness by 0.15mm to address the leakage complaints noted
in the Q1 CAPA. Bench testing shows the change eliminates leakage up to
40 psi, well above the 25 psi worst-case infusion pressure spec. Regulatory
confirmed this is a minor change not requiring a new 510(k), but does
require an updated risk assessment per ISO 14971.

Action items: Arjun to complete the updated risk assessment by next Friday.
Priya to release updated drawings to Manufacturing for pilot lot build.
Meera to update the technical file and notify the EU authorized
representative of the change.

Design Review Meeting Notes - ECO-4471 Follow-Up
Date: 2024-05-02

Attendees: R&D Lead (Priya), Quality Engineer (Arjun), Manufacturing Engineer (Farhan)

Discussion: Pilot lot (50 units) of the revised luer connector completed
first-article inspection with zero dimensional non-conformances. Burst
pressure testing on the pilot lot averaged 41.2 psi, consistent with bench
data. Farhan raised a concern that the thicker wall section increases
injection molding cycle time by approximately 4 seconds per part; Priya
will evaluate whether a minor gate redesign can recover the cycle time
without affecting the wall thickness change.

Action items: Farhan to run a full production-scale trial of 500 units.
Arjun to close the updated risk assessment following pilot lot results.
Priya to evaluate the gate redesign option and report back by May 16.

Design Review Meeting Notes - ECO-4471 Closure
Date: 2024-05-30

Attendees: R&D Lead (Priya), Quality Engineer (Arjun), Regulatory Affairs (Meera), Manufacturing Engineer (Farhan)

Discussion: Production-scale trial of 500 units passed all acceptance
criteria with zero leakage failures. The gate redesign recovered the

wall thickness. The updated risk assessment was approved by Arjun and
concurred with by Meera. Change is approved for release into standard
production beginning with the June 2024 lot.
